🌐 SUBMIT REMOTE TESTIMONY

The NH House allows citizens to submit written testimony and sign in for hearings remotely via the official General Court portal. You can indicate your position (For / Against / Neutral) on any bill and upload a written statement — all without leaving home.

  • Available for all NH House Committee hearings
  • Sign-in closes when the hearing begins — submit early
  • Written testimony must be a PDF, max 10MB
  • You do not need to create an account to submit

📝 TIPS FOR EFFECTIVE TESTIMONY

Whether written or in-person, your testimony is most impactful when it's personal and specific.

  • State your name, town, and connection to the issue up front
  • Keep it focused — one or two key points land better than ten
  • Use a personal story or local example if you have one
  • Be respectful even when you strongly disagree — it builds credibility
  • Written testimony submitted before the hearing is read into the record

JOHN M. FORMELLA

Attorney General of New Hampshire

John Formella has served as NH Attorney General since April 2021, first appointed by Gov. Chris Sununu and reconfirmed unanimously by the Executive Council in September 2025 under Gov. Kelly Ayotte for a second term running to 2029.

He earned his B.S. and M.S. from Florida State University and his J.D. from George Washington University Law School. Before becoming AG, he served as Governor Sununu's legal counsel.

Republican In Office Since 2021 Term Ends 2029

📋 KEY PRIORITIES & RECORD

Under AG Formella, the NH Department of Justice has focused on several major areas:

  • Public Safety: Led major prosecutions of violent offenders; expanded the state's Cold Case Unit; launched the Northern Border Alliance Task Force to combat fentanyl trafficking along NH's northern border.
  • Youth Justice: Oversaw the state's response to lawsuits from victims of alleged abuse at the Youth Detention Center (YDC).
  • Consumer Protection: Joined multi-state lawsuits against Purdue Pharma over opioid crisis liability and against Meta for making social media addictive to children.
  • Second Amendment: Advocated for gun owners' rights in NH and filed briefs in federal cases protecting law-abiding gun owners.
  • National Leadership: Served as President of the National Association of Attorneys General (NAAG) in 2025.
